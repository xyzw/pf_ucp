# Lean development for pf_ucp.tex

This Lean development checks the principal physical continuation and Hohenberg–Kohn results of `../pf_ucp.tex` for both the spinful Pauli Hamiltonian and the spinless Hamiltonian `H_sl`. The paper states the spinless versions as the final clauses of `ucp-main-theorem` and `thm-hk-nonvanishing`. The spinful current is the actual Pauli velocity current, identified with `jᶜ = jᵖ + jᵖʰ + ½ curl m`. The HK endpoints include both cutoff alternatives, exceptional sets, explicit weak-phase relations and actual quotient classes. Both the spinless and the spinful zero-coupling counterexamples are also checked.

The finite-spin extension constructs the manuscript's Pauli form on actual photon-valued spinors and proves its matrix, gradient, current, localization and variational identities. It derives the multiplier bounds from the coefficient assumptions, establishes the common closed form domain including the joint antisymmetric sector, and identifies the local operator domain. The weak Pauli-square chain treats the distributional classical curl as a form on H¹ before constructing its L² source on the comparison domain. The full Pauli Carleman estimate, including a fixed energy shift and both magnetic terms, is also checked. The spinful continuation chain is checked, including Caccioppoli estimates, infinite-order vanishing, strong continuation, almost-everywhere nonvanishing and spin-summed density positivity. It includes the exceptional-set and variational ground-state endpoints. The integrated Pauli HK assembly is checked, including the current identity, external-energy decomposition, phase and photon variations, common ground states and exact vector and scalar uniqueness. Verification evidence is recorded below.

Both the spinful theorem and its spinless analogue have endpoints on actual gauge quotient classes. The witness formulations spell out `A₂ = A₁ − ∇θ` and `v₁ = v₂ + (E₁ − E₂)/N` almost everywhere. Local regularity is required for only one chosen potential pair.

The development uses Lean 4.34.0-rc2 and a pinned mathlib revision. This folder is independent of the sibling `gpt_lean/` project. The 679 required `GptLean` source modules are included locally, with their original names and unchanged contents. [Vendoring details](vendor/README.md) record the selection and provenance. The new proofs contain no placeholders or new axioms. The physical results keep standard analytic backgrounds as explicit parameters.
