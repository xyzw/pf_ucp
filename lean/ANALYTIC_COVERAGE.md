# Analytic coverage of the companion paper

This record distinguishes the proved endpoints from separately unpackaged statements in `../pf_ucp.tex`. The physical continuation and Hohenberg–Kohn endpoints cover both the spinful Pauli Hamiltonian and the spinless Hamiltonian `H_sl`. The paper states the spinless versions as the final clauses of `ucp-main-theorem` and `thm-hk-nonvanishing`. The spinful current `jᶜ = jᵖ + jᵖʰ + ½ curl m` is identified through its actual distributional magnetization term. The integrated pure-state HK theorem is checked for both cutoff alternatives, including exceptional sets, explicit weak-phase relations and actual quotient classes. The spinful zero-coupling counterexample is also checked on the actual Pauli form. The detailed proof scope appears in [README.md](README.md#spinful-hohenbergkohn-assembly), with verification evidence below. This record does not replace the dependency audit.

For the orbital model, the physical UCP chain includes the fermionic form reduction and the bridge from the existing variational ground states. The Hohenberg–Kohn conclusion is assembled for both cutoff alternatives, including an arbitrary nonzero cutoff class when the actual common density has an exponential moment. The full spinless zero-coupling counterexample is also assembled, with common ground-state uniqueness, physical density-current data and gauge inequivalence. The orbital exceptional-set UCP and HK extensions and the illustrative singular potential are also proved. The formulation on full weak-gauge quotient classes is proved for both the global and exceptional-set regularity packages. All nine gauge modules compile without errors or warnings, and the recorded aggregate audit passes. The precise proof boundary and remaining work are described below.
